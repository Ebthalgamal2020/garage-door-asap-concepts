Urgent — call us now Likely fix: Spring Replacement

Sounds like a broken spring

Springs carry the weight of the door. A loud bang followed by a door that won’t open — or feels incredibly heavy — usually means a spring has snapped.

Safety tip: Don’t try to lift the door or keep pressing the opener. Without a working spring, the door’s full weight is unsupported.
