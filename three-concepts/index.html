Garage Door ASAP Service

Homepage redesign · Client review · October 2026

Three homepage concepts for Garage Door ASAP.

Each one is a complete, working homepage in the brand’s own red, charcoal and Poppins, with the client’s real photos, verified business details and genuine Google reviews. Emergency calls are always one tap away, and kept separate from non-urgent quote requests.

V1

The Lift

Light, fluid and friendly. Its one shape is the bend of a garage-door track: it curves the section edges, frames the client’s photo in an arch the door rolls up into, and carries the “How it works” line. Its fix finder groups problems by urgency and links a side-view door diagram to the likely repair, with a before/after slider of a real job and Mr. ASAP as the friendly face.

V2

The Signature

Bright, clean and friendly. A white and light-grey page with the brand red used only as a confident accent, Mr. ASAP as the star, and truck-style sticker badges. A “What’s happening?” problem list shows the fix, a real photo and Mr. ASAP’s safety tip. It also has the real crew and their trucks, a before/after slider, a spring-life estimator, the 29-point tune-up, reviews, city search and a two-step quote.

V3

The Garage Door Lab

An interactive, customer-focused service site. A “Find my fix” tool sits in the full-width photo hero: pick what your door is doing and an illustrated door shows the part, then Mr. ASAP gives a safety tip and two quick questions route you to “Call now” or “Book a visit”. Urgent repairs lead an asymmetric services layout, and a refined “Know your door” guide explains each part.
