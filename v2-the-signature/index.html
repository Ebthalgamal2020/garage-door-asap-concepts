      FAQ

      How quickly can you get to me?

      We offer same-day service across Central Florida and 24/7 emergency service. Arrival time depends on where you are and how busy the day is, so call (877) 787-7121 and we’ll give you a time on the phone.

      Do you really work 24 hours a day?

      Yes: 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including weekends and holidays. A door that won’t close is a security problem, and we treat it as one.

      Is it the spring or the opener?

      A loud bang, a visible gap in the spring above the door, or a door that suddenly feels very heavy usually means a broken spring. If the motor hums but the door won’t move, it can be either. Your technician confirms the cause on site.

      How long do garage door springs last?

      Springs are rated in cycles (one open and one close). The minimum spec is 10,000 cycles and most last 10,000–20,000. The springs we use last up to 50,000 cycles. Estimate yours.

      What affects the price of a repair?

      Mainly the parts needed and the size and type of your door. Your technician explains the options and the price before work starts. The measure and quote are free, our prices are guaranteed, and financing is available with no payment and no interest for 25 months.

      Are your repairs guaranteed?

      Yes. Every job comes with our 100% satisfaction guarantee and a lifetime warranty on all parts. We’re licensed, bonded and insured.
