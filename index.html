The Lift

Light, fluid and friendly. Its one shape is the bend of a garage-door track: it curves the section edges, frames the client’s photo in an arch the door rolls up into, and carries the “How it works” line. Its fix finder groups problems by urgency and links a side-view door diagram to the likely repair. It also has a before/after slider of a real job, with Mr. ASAP as the friendly face.

The Garage Door Lab

An interactive, customer-focused service site. A “Find my fix” tool sits in the full-width photo hero: pick what your door is doing and an illustrated door shows the part, then Mr. ASAP gives a safety tip and two quick questions route you to “Call now” or “Book a visit”. Urgent repairs lead an asymmetric services layout, and a refined “Know your door” guide explains each part. The client’s own crooked-door video loads only when tapped.
