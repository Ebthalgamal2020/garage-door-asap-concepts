    How quickly can you get to me?

    We offer same-day service across Central Florida and 24/7 emergency service. Arrival time depends on where you are and how busy the day is, so call (877) 787-7121 and we’ll give you a time on the phone.

    Do you really work 24 hours a day?

    Yes. We’re open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including weekends and holidays. A door that won’t close is a security problem, so we treat it as one.

    Is it the spring or the opener?

    A loud bang from the garage, a visible gap in the spring above the door, or a door that feels extremely heavy usually means a broken spring. If the motor hums but the door won’t move, it can be either. If the door reverses before closing, check that nothing is blocking the safety sensors. Either way, our technician will diagnose it on site.

    What affects the price of a repair?

    The parts needed (for example, one spring or two, or new cables and rollers), and the size and type of your door. Your technician explains the options and price before work starts. We offer a free measure and quote, guarantee our prices, and offer financing with no payment and no interest for 25 months.

    Are your repairs guaranteed?

    Yes. Every job comes with our 100% satisfaction guarantee and a lifetime warranty on all parts. We’re licensed, bonded and insured.

    Which areas do you cover?

    We’re based in Clermont and serve 73 cities across Orange, Seminole, Osceola, Lake, Volusia and Pinellas counties. Check your city, or call us if you’re nearby. We may still be able to help.
